# Territory coverage follow-ons

[Coverage reporting and audit](../current/territory-coverage.md) are built. These concerns remain outside that implementation:

- **Canon authoring and command trees.** The canon's [command-tree audit card](../../../terminus-canon/docs/future/roadmap/territory-audit-command-trees.md) owns surveying projects, judging omissions, and any authoring convention about invocation surfaces. The audit and optional map supply evidence; they do not make those judgments. Terminus's own rubric currently contains only general qualities, so its coverage and broker invariants still need deliberate project-local canon work.
- **Review participation.** The [changeset-exclusion proposal](terminus-changeset-exclusions.md) concerns which files enter review. Coverage exclusions only suppress gap reports and must not acquire those execution semantics.
- **Multiple passes.** The [multiple-review-passes card](roadmap/multiple-review-passes.md) owns aggregation. A later cycle model must define file-level coverage deliberately; the union of selected qualities cannot establish it.
- **Automatic policy.** Automatic widening, coverage-based blocking, canon repair, scores, and low-coverage thresholds remain deferred. Territory reach cannot establish which invariant is missing or appropriate, and quality counts do not measure invariant completeness. Any future policy needs a separate design decision.
